❔ Dashboard Guide

Dishes & Menu

Adding a Dish

Navigate to "Dishes" and click "+ Add Dish". Fill out the required details (Name, Price, Category).

Managing Stock

Toggle the "Available / Sold Out" switch next to any dish. Items marked as "Sold Out" will automatically reset to "Available" at midnight (IST).

Bestsellers

Check the "★ Bestseller" box when adding/editing a dish. It will get a special badge and appear in the "Bestsellers" category at the top of the customer menu.

Adding Tags/Subtext

Use the "Subtext / Tag" field to add extra information below the description, like "↳ + ₹40 Extra for Desi Ghee".

Categories

Managing Categories

Go to the "Categories" tab. You can add new categories or edit existing ones. Empty categories will not be shown to customers.

Sales & Bulk Manager

Running a Sale

Select a category (or 'All Categories'), enter a discount percentage (e.g., 10), and a badge text (e.g., "10% OFF"). The system calculates the discounted price, strikes out the old price, and adds the badge automatically.

Ending a Sale

Use the "End Active Sales" tool. It removes the badge and restores the original pricing instantly.

Find & Replace Pricing

Quickly update prices in bulk. Enter the old price (e.g., 70) and the new price (e.g., 80) to update all matching items at once.

Bulk Stock Update

Quickly mark an entire category (or all items) as "Available" or "Sold Out".

Settings & Team

Global Banner

Add a message in the "Global Banner" field to display an announcement at the very top of the customer menu.

Managing Staff

Authorize new admin accounts by entering an email and password. They can use these credentials to log into this dashboard. You can revoke access at any time.

Account Recovery Guide

For Staff: Please contact the restaurant owner. They can easily revoke your old email and authorize a new one directly from their dashboard.

For the Primary Owner: If you lose access to your primary email, you are locked out of Firebase password resets. To regain access, you must manually force a new email into the database:

  1. Log into your Cloudflare Dashboard.
  2. Navigate to D1 SQL Database → anna-menu-db → Console.
  3. Copy, paste, and execute this exact SQL command:
    INSERT INTO authorized_admins (email, added_at) VALUES ('your_new_email@gmail.com', datetime('now'));
  4. Go to your Firebase Console → Authentication → Add User, and create a password for that same new email address.
